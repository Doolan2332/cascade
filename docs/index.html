What Do I Do?

Cascade compares two decklists, prices and charts the difference, and turns the new cards into a print-ready proxy sheet. Here are the two most common ways people use it, plus how the print preview works.

Upgrading a Precon

Turn a stock preconstructed deck into your own build, and see exactly what changed.

  1. Search for the precon's commander in Commander Lookup up top — or pick one from Popular Commanders, or roll 🎲.
  2. Under Precon Decklist, click Load into Original. This pulls the actual stock 100-card list straight in.
  3. Paste (or type) your upgraded 100 cards into the Updated box.
  4. Click Compare lists. The Cut and Added columns show exactly what changed, with price totals and a color identity shift chart underneath.

Building a New Deck Around a Commander

No list yet? Start from what's actually being played.

  1. Search for or pick your commander the same way as above.
  2. Under EDHREC Average Deck, choose a power bracket and budget if you like, then click Load into Updated.
  3. Leave Original empty and click Compare lists — every card shows up as "Added," giving you a full, priced decklist to start building from.

The Print Preview (Proxy Sheet)

Once you have Added cards, click Preview & Print PDF to open a print-ready sheet you can fine-tune before printing.

  • Search — the first box adds any card by name; the second adds tokens, emblems, or similar indicators by name.
  • Rearrange — drag any card to a new spot in the sheet.
  • Alternate art — click a card to browse every printing on Scryfall and swap in a different set or art.
  • Tokens and emblems a card creates (Treasure, Beast, Clue, Monarch…) are pulled in automatically.
  • When it's ready, click Print / Save as PDF — it opens your browser's print dialog, landscape, 8 cards per page, with cut lines.

A Few More Things

  • Click any card's name to see its full text, rulings, and every other printing with prices.
  • History (top right) saves every comparison you run, so you can revisit past sessions.
